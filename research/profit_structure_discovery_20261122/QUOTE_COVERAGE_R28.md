## Interpretation and limits

- The 2024 two-tick segment is positive (+17.955U, ROI +1.2323%, n1457), whereas all four historical tick groups were positive due to training selection. This is **not** independent validation of a tick-count filter.

- Quote tick count may reflect retrospective data capture, not contemporaneous 2024 availability; first quote time being earlier than selected quote time does not prove historical tradeability.
- Descriptive subgroup bands are not approved gates or live filters; 2025-2026 remain untouched; M8, Telegram and production unchanged.
